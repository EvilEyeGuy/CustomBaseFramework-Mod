Categories

A category groups pieces, for example HouseSmall, Workshop or Military. Settlements order categories, not single pieces, and the generator picks a piece from the category. Placement rules live on the category, so every piece in it follows them.

  1. List of categories with the number of pieces. The chips show all, only used or only empty ones.
  2. Tiles of every piece in the category. Click one to open it in the Piece tab.
  3. Rules on the right, in sections.
  4. Used in lists every settlement that orders this category. Click a line to open the settlement.

Inheritance

A category can have a Parent. Two things follow from it:

  • Rules go down. A rule set to inherits takes the value of the parent, and so on up the chain. The tooltip says where a value comes from.
  • Pieces go up. A piece in a child category also counts as a piece of the parent. A settlement that orders the parent may get pieces of the children.
  • Merging stays within the own category. Two houses share a wall only if their own first category is the same. A parent never joins them: a brewery and a tannery ordered through Workshop stay apart.

Placement

  • Road access: must be reachable from the road network.
  • Unique: at most one per settlement.
  • Outside only: never stands inside the walls. Outside allowed: may also stand outside them.
  • Landmark: placed early and in plain view.
  • Wall contact: pieces of this category prefer to stand against the town wall. The piece field At the wall controls this more finely and wins.
  • Weight: how often pieces of this category are drawn when several fit.

Preferences

Values from 0 to 1: how strongly pieces want to be near the gate, the center, the wall or on the main road. Spread makes buildings of the category avoid each other.

Blocks

  • Block limit: how many buildings of this category at most form one block, wall to wall. Empty means 3.
  • Merge: whether buildings join their neighbors into one block. Yes forces it, No forbids it.

Annexes

An annex is a small piece that docks onto a house: a stable, a woodshed, a beer garden.

  1. Annex categories: which annexes may attach to pieces of this category. Empty takes the list of the parent.
  2. Chance per anchor: the chance that an annex is built at one annex anchor of the house. Default 0.6.
  3. At most: annexes per house. Default 2.
  4. Repeatable: set on the category of the annex. Yes allows the same annex several times on one house.

The house marks where annexes go with a Connect anchor named Anbau on its outer wall. The annex has one too, on the shared wall or one cell outside its shape when it only touches the house. A weight below 1 on the annex is also the chance that it is tried at all.
