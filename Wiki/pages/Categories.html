Categories

Categories

A category is a group of pieces of the same sort, for example HouseSmall, Workshop or Military. Settlements ask for categories, not for single pieces, and the generator picks a piece from the category each time. That is why a category with many pieces gives more variety. Placement rules are set on the category, so every piece in it follows them.

  1. List of categories with the number of pieces in each. The chips show All, only Used categories (those with at least one piece) or only Empty ones.
  2. Tiles of every piece in the category. Click one to open it in the Piece tab.
  3. Rules on the right, in sections. Save at the bottom writes them into the category's file.
  4. Used in lists every settlement that names this category in a district or in its fortification. Click a line to open the settlement.

Inheritance

A category can have a Parent, another category it belongs under. This has three effects:

  • Rules go down. A rule set to inherits, or a number field left empty, takes the value of the parent, and so on up the chain. The tooltip says where a value comes from. The chain of parents is shown at the bottom left.
  • Pieces go up. A piece in a child category also counts as a piece of the parent. A settlement that asks for the parent may get pieces of the children.
  • Merging stays within the own category. Two houses share a wall only if their own first category is the same. A parent never joins them: a brewery and a tannery ordered through Workshop stay apart.

Placement

  • Road access: buildings of this category must be reachable from the road network.
  • Needs no road: only takes effect when Road access is No. Buildings of this category then need no road and may be entered from every side, like a farm or a market. In XML this is the zone Outside; the zones Inside and Center do nothing.
  • Wall contact: pieces of this category prefer to stand against the town wall. The piece field At the wall controls this more finely and wins.
  • Weight: how often pieces of this category are drawn when several fit. 1 is normal. For annexes, a value below 1 is also the chance that the annex is tried at all.

How many buildings of a category a settlement gets is set by its entries in the Settlement tab.

Location

Where buildings stand in the town is set per district in the Settlement tab, see Settlements. A district is a group of buildings in one part of the town. Categories have no location values.

Blocks

A block is a row of buildings that stand wall to wall.

  • Block limit: how many buildings of this category at most form one block. Empty means 3.
  • Merge: whether buildings of this category join their neighbours into one block. Yes forces it, No forbids it.

The generator reads both values only from the building's own first category, not from its parents.

Annexes

An annex is a small piece that docks onto a house: a stable, a woodshed, a beer garden. The fields below decide which annexes a house gets and how many.

  1. Annex categories: which annexes may attach to pieces of this category. Empty takes the list of the parent.
  2. Chance per anchor: the chance that an annex is built at one annex anchor of the house. Default 0.6.
  3. At most: annexes per house. Default 2.
  4. Repeatable: set on the category of the annex. Yes allows the same annex several times on one house.

Full wall and Overhang are also set on the category of the annex. See the settings table on Annexes.

The house marks where annexes go with a Connect anchor named Annex on its outer wall. The annex has one too. How the generator places them and what an annex needs is explained on Annexes.
