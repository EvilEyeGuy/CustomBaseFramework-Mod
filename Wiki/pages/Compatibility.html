Compatibility

Content for other mods

Custom Base Framework brings its own buildings and styles for some mods. They load only when that mod is active.

Mod Folder Content
Medieval Overhaul 1.6/Mods/MedievalOverhaul Styles, pieces, object lists and loot for medieval towns
Vanilla Factions Expanded - Medieval 2 1.6/Mods/VFEMedieval2 Styles and pieces built from the base game and VFE Medieval 2
none 1.6/Defs Vanilla style for modern towns, base game only

Some settlement plans serve every faction of a tech level. Medieval factions from other mods get medieval towns, modern factions get modern towns, without patches.

Multiplayer

The planner is deterministic. It stops after a counted amount of work, not after a time limit. With Multiplayer (rwmt.Multiplayer) it runs without a clock, and the same seed gives the same settlement on every machine.

Mods that reshape the map

Map Designer and Geological Landforms work together with CBF. They shape every other map as usual, but they do not act while CBF generates a settlement, so rivers, cliffs and terrain features do not cut through the town.

Mods with settlements of their own

World Domination 2.0

World Domination turns the world into a living map, similar to RimWar with outposts:

  • Faction settlements have a tier: T1 outpost, T2 town, T3 fortress, T4 citadel.
  • They gain strength every day, grow, found new settlements, build roads, send traders and raid each other and you.
  • Under pressure, factions pack weak settlements into a T4 hub. T4 settlements can have mortars and anti-air.
  • When you attack, the garrison scales with the current strength of the settlement.
  • World Domination uses its own layouts for assault maps only for factions switched on in World Setup under Allegiances.

By default CBF builds these settlements and picks the settlement type by tier, see Settlement types:

  • Among the settlement types the faction may get anyway (tech level and faction rules unchanged), CBF takes the one whose WD tier matches, otherwise the closest one.
  • If a settlement grows to a higher tier in World Domination, its type changes as long as its map has not been generated yet.
  • Settlement types assigned by hand in the world editor stay as they are.

The shipped types: village 1, market town 2, large market town 3, fortress city 4, for Medieval Overhaul and VFE Medieval 2 alike. Modern town 1 to 2, large town 3, metropolis 4. The three modern types serve industrial, spacer and ultra factions alike, the garrison picks spacer units by tech level.

Build World Domination settlements with CBF in the mod settings switches this. Off: CBF leaves World Domination settlements of factions without layouts of their own alone. The setting and the WD tier field in the Settlement tab only appear when World Domination is active.

Empire

Vassal colonies of Empire get no CBF settlements, are no quest givers for CBF quests and are not listed under settlements per faction.

Other mods with their own map generator

Settlements that another mod builds with its own map generator keep it. CBF then also leaves their map size and world map icon alone.

Both are part of Leave settlements of other settlement mods alone in the mod settings, on by default.
