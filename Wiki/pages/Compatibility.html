Compatibility

Compatibility

CBF integrates other mods in two ways: optional content uses their buildings and items, while generation integrations coordinate which system creates a settlement map. The sections below explain what CBF supplies, what the other mod retains and which settings control the handover.

Content for other mods

Custom Base Framework (CBF) brings its own buildings and settlement types for some mods, made from the things of those mods. Each set loads only when its mod is active. The folder column names where the files lie in the CBF mod folder.

Mod Folder Content
Medieval Overhaul 1.6/Mods/MedievalOverhaul Village, market town, large market town, fortress and fortress city, a tribal camp for neolithic factions, and camps for quest sites. Houses, workshops, castle, churches, military buildings, market squares, tournament ground, small gap houses for narrow lanes. Huts get wood stoves and changing beds (straw, fur, wicker, log). Two quests, see Quests.
Vanilla Factions Expanded - Medieval 2 1.6/Mods/VFEMedieval2 Village, market town, large market town and fortress city, with buildings adapted from the Medieval Overhaul set using base game and VFE Medieval 2 objects. Works without Medieval Overhaul. Living houses use the self-refilling shelf of VFE Medieval 2, armories and treasuries have shelves with their own loot tables.
none 1.6/Defs Modern village, modern town, large town and metropolis from base game things only, for industrial, spacer and ultra factions, and camps for quest sites. Spacer defence nests and bunkers (the mech shield only with Royalty), two fallback gatehouses of stone and wood, modern wall profiles with sandbag lines.
World Domination 2.0 1.6/Mods/WorldDomination, WorldDomination_MO, WorldDomination_VFEM2 Outposts for the World Domination types: farm, logging and mining camps, production and slave villages, war camps. Modern ones always, medieval ones with Medieval Overhaul or VFE Medieval 2, see Settlement types. Their buildings (granary, sawmill, charcoal kiln, smelter, barracks, slave pen, command tent and more) also load without World Domination.
Dark Ages: Crypts and Tombs inside the Medieval Overhaul and VFE Medieval 2 pieces Three extra cemeteries each for Medieval Overhaul and VFE Medieval 2, with sarcophagi, burial urns, gargoyles, heroic memorials, sepulchral braziers and candles from Dark Ages. With Dark Ages active they replace the plain cemeteries; without it the plain ones are built.
A Rim of Ice and Fire 1.6/Mods/RIAF, RIAF_MO Holdings of the houses built by CBF: hamlet, small market town and small fortress for Medieval Overhaul, on 150 maps. See A Rim of Ice and Fire.

Some settlement types serve every faction of a tech level. Medieval factions from other mods therefore get medieval towns and modern factions get modern towns, without patches. See Settlement types for the full list.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer needs every player's game to build the same settlement. The planner that lays out a settlement is deterministic: it stops after a counted amount of work, not after a time limit. With Multiplayer (rwmt.Multiplayer) active, the planner avoids time-based stopping. Matching inputs and content are still required; this behaviour alone does not verify every editor action or interaction with other mods in multiplayer.

Mods that reshape the map

Map Designer and Geological Landforms work together with CBF. They shape every other map as usual, but they do not act while CBF generates a settlement, so rivers, cliffs and terrain features do not cut through the town.

Mods with settlements of their own

World Domination 2.0

World Domination 2.0 (WD) turns the world into a living map, similar to RimWar with outposts:

  • Faction settlements have a tier: T1 outpost, T2 town, T3 fortress, T4 citadel.
  • They gain strength every day, grow, found new settlements, build roads, send traders and raid each other and you.
  • Under pressure, factions pack weak settlements into a T4 hub. T4 settlements can have mortars and anti-air.
  • Your own World Domination outposts (farm, mine, research and so on) have no map of their own. When one is attacked, World Domination builds a fixed defence map. CBF leaves these alone.

Who does what

World Domination keeps running the world: tiers, strength, growth, sabotage, mortars, reinforcements from nearby settlements and conquest. CBF only builds the map of a settlement when you first arrive there: the layout, the defenders and the loot. CBF uses World Domination strength and loot data alongside its own plans. The garrison rules and loot slider below determine the resulting encounter.

Build World Domination settlements with CBF in the mod settings switches this, on by default. Off: CBF leaves World Domination settlements alone. CBF also respects the map generation switch that World Domination has per faction under Allegiances in World Setup. The setting and the WD fields in the Settlement tab only appear when World Domination is active.

Which settlement type is built

  • CBF only picks among the settlement types the faction may get anyway. Tech level and faction rules stay the same.
  • Among these it takes the one whose WD tier (set in the Settlement tab) matches the settlement, otherwise the closest one.
  • Every World Domination settlement also has a type that depends on its tier: logging, mining or farming camp at tier 1, production or slave village at tier 2, town at tier 3, citadel at tier 4, and vanguard camps at any tier. Among the plans of the right tier, CBF prefers those whose WD type lists this type, then those without a type.
  • If a settlement grows to a higher tier, its type changes as long as its map has not been generated yet.
  • Settlement types assigned by hand in the world editor stay as they are.

The WD tiers of the shipped types:

  • Medieval Overhaul and VFE Medieval 2: village 1, market town 2, large market town 3, fortress city 4. The Medieval Overhaul fortress is tier 3.
  • Modern: modern village 1, modern town 2, large town 3, metropolis 4. They serve industrial, spacer and ultra factions alike; the garrison picks spacer units by tech level.
  • Outposts for the World Domination types come on top, see Settlement types.

Defenders

The garrison of a CBF settlement follows its World Domination strength. A settlement at full strength has the defenders of its settlement type. A weakened one has fewer, but never less than a minimum per tier:

Tier Minimum
T1 50 %
T2 60 %
T3 75 %
T4 90 %

A settlement that escalated late in the game gets up to 25 % more defenders.

Loot

World Domination has a loot budget per tier, counted in market value in silver: T1 1650, T2 4050, T3 6500, T4 9750. The goods come from its own tables for the settlement's type (farming, logging, mining, fortress, citadel, otherwise a default table per tier). The strength of the settlement does not change the budget.

CBF fills the containers of a World Domination settlement in this order:

  1. Themed containers keep their own CBF loot table: armouries, churches, libraries and the like. Together they may use up to half of the budget.
  2. General storage gets the rest of the budget: chests, crates, sacks, barrels and empty shelves whose loot table counts as general storage (the storage, hut, house, kitchen, farm and pantry tables of CBF). Instead of their CBF table they get goods from the World Domination table, rolled by its weights. Containers are picked at random across the whole settlement, one stack per container and pass, at most 3000 silver per stack, until the budget is used up.
  3. Medieval Overhaul containers that show their content (wine racks, treasure shelves, ore barrels, weapon racks, market stalls) keep Medieval Overhaul's own loot. World Domination goods would not match their look.

When the budget is used up, the remaining general containers stay empty and show their empty look. If CBF cannot read the World Domination tables, the normal CBF loot is used.

Loot in World Domination settlements in the mod settings mixes both kinds, 50% by default:

Setting Result
100% Every container keeps its planned CBF or Medieval Overhaul loot. World Domination loot is off.
50% About half of the containers keep their planned loot. The others share the full World Domination budget, evenly, and each gets at least one item.
0% As described above: the general containers get the full World Domination budget.

The World Domination budget is never reduced: below 100% you always find at least as much as at 0%, plus the planned loot of the containers that keep it. The share is drawn per container, so the mix differs from settlement to settlement.

No WD loot in the Settlement tab switches World Domination loot off for that settlement type, whatever the slider says. For modders: a loot table with wdBudget set to true counts as general storage, see the field reference.

The map

  • World Domination reveals the whole map of its own settlements on entry. It does not do this for CBF settlements. You explore them like any other map.
  • After the fight, Search site under Orders marks every closed container for opening, see Settlement types.

Empire

Vassal colonies of Empire get no CBF settlements, are no quest givers for CBF quests and are not listed under settlements per faction.

A Rim of Ice and Fire

CBF builds the settlements of all factions, including the eight castle factions of A Rim of Ice and Fire. Their RIAF castles are not generated. RIAF's own places (fiefs, banner calls, duels, ruins) stay with RIAF.

Only the seats of the first faction of each house get a large type, meaning one with WD tier 3 or 4 (large market town, fortress or fortress city). Seats of further factions of the same house become villages or market towns. Seat names stay as they are.

The villages, towns and garrisons of the houses (holdings) are built by CBF too, when a settlement type with a matching RIAF holding (set in the Settlement tab) exists. CBF builds the houses and walls. Defenders, levies, residents and stores stay with A Rim of Ice and Fire; the stores go to a free spot near the map centre. Without a matching type, A Rim of Ice and Fire builds the holding itself. CBF ships a hamlet, a small market town and a small fortress for Medieval Overhaul, made for maps of 150 cells.

Switch it off with Build A Rim of Ice and Fire holdings with CBF in the mod settings.

Other mods with their own map generator

Settlements that another mod builds with its own map generator keep it. CBF then also leaves their map size and world map icon alone. This is part of Leave settlements of other settlement mods alone in the mod settings, on by default.
