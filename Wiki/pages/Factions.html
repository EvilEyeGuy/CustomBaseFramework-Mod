Factions

Which faction gets which settlement

A plan reaches a faction in one of two ways:

  1. The plan names the faction or its faction category under Factions.
  2. The plan ticks For all factions of these levels and the faction's tech level is among them.

A plan that names no faction and does not tick For all factions of these levels is only used for places without a faction, such as quest sites.

A faction that has plans of its own through the first way never gets the general plans of the second. Excluded keeps a faction away from a plan in any case.

Faction overview

Faction overview below the settlement list shows every faction in the game with the plans it gets and the chance of each one. Click a faction to open its window: assign plans, change their weight, and save all changed plans at once.

Takeover

Every faction that has a matching plan gets its bases from the generator. No patch is needed. Only the settlement step of the map is swapped, so faction bases keep their rocks and power lines.

  • A faction with no matching plan gets RimWorld's own settlement, never an empty map.
  • Players can switch single factions back to RimWorld's own bases in the mod settings, see Mod settings.
  • Merge patches in the settlement's General section, row Takeover, combines patch files the editor wrote into one.
