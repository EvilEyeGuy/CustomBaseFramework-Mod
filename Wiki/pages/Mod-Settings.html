Mod settings

Options > Mod settings > Custom Base Framework.

Editor

  • Editor in the architect menu shows the editor as its own category in the architect menu. Without it the editor opens only in development mode, debug menu entry Open editor.
  • Open editor guide opens this guide. In the editor, Guide at the bottom of the left bar and in the title bar does the same.

Settlements per faction

Lists every faction that gets its bases from Custom Base Framework, from any mod. Choose factions shows all of them in a window of their own:

  • Search by faction or mod name.
  • Sorted by mod or Sorted by name changes the order.
  • All on and All off switch every faction at once.

Untick a faction to give it RimWorld's own bases again. Bases that were already visited stay as they are.

Building ground

Reserve building ground before the rock: before RimWorld places the rock, the edge of the mountain recedes where the settlement will stand, only as far as it needs to fit. Off means the settlement has to make do with the terrain as grown and can fail on rocky tiles.

Other settlement mods

  • Leave settlements of other settlement mods alone, on by default. Settlements that another mod builds with its own map generator keep it. Vassal colonies of Empire get no CBF settlements and no CBF quests.
  • Build World Domination settlements with CBF, on by default, only shown with World Domination active. CBF replaces the World Domination layouts and picks the settlement type by tier. Off: CBF leaves them alone.

See Compatibility.

Make everything buildable

Gives every building without an architect category one, so it can be placed from the architect menu instead of crafted and installed. Things without build cost are skipped. Takes effect after a restart. Open build orders for these buildings are cancelled when you switch it off or remove CBF, and the materials are returned.

Error report

If a settlement cannot be generated, CBF writes a report with the terrain and the log, and a vanilla settlement is placed instead. A window opens and offers to upload the report.

  • Upload latest error report in the settings does the same later.
  • Uploaded are two files: Bericht.txt (mod list, settlement type, seed, log excerpt) and Gelaende.gel (the terrain of the map). No save games.
  • The link is public. The files are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Copy links copies the links together with a short summary of the error. Post them in a comment on the Steam Workshop page.

Remove CBF from this save

Remove CBF data from this save is available in a loaded game. It removes CBF quests, map data, containers and settlement plans. Settlements remain, containers are emptied onto the ground. It cannot be undone.

Afterwards save at once, quit the game and only then remove the mod. As long as CBF stays active, the world map and new maps bring CBF data back.
