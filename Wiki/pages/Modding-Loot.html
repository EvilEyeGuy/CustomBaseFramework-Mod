Modding: loot, object lists, containers and crops

Modding

This page covers the XML side of loot in Custom Base Framework (CBF). The editor side is described on Loot. Every field is listed on the field reference pages linked at the end.

For editor terminology, see the guide overview. To change an existing definition, use a patch; the XML below explains the underlying data.

How loot gets into a settlement

For new pieces, use a Fill anchor to connect an object list with a loot table. Older content zones remain supported and are described below.

  1. A piece has a legend entry with anchorKind Fill (shown as Loot in the editor) or Object.
  2. The anchor names an object list in objects and, for Fill anchors, a loot table in lootTable.
  3. CBF picks one structure from the object list that fits the anchor area and places it.
  4. The loot table is rolled and the items go into that structure or onto it.

Two shipped anchors from a Medieval Overhaul piece:

<li><symbol>4</symbol><anchor>Barrel</anchor><anchorKind>Fill</anchorKind><objects>CBF_MO_Barrel</objects><lootTable>CBF_MO_Loot_Larder</lootTable></li>
<li><symbol>5</symbol><anchor>Shelf</anchor><anchorKind>Fill</anchorKind><objects>CBF_MO_HeraldryShelf</objects><lootForeign>true</lootForeign></li>

The first places a barrel or crate and fills it with larder loot. The second places a Medieval Overhaul shelf and lets that mod fill it.

Node Meaning
anchorKind Fill places a structure and loot. Object places a structure only. The anchor's lootTable is ignored.
objects defName of a LootBuildingCategoryDef.
lootTable defName of a LootTableDef. Used for every structure of the list unless a member brings its own table.
lootForeign The anchor places the structure and leaves filling to the mod that owns it. The anchor's lootTable is ignored.
chance Chance that the anchor is used at all. If it fails on an anchor with an object list, the anchor cells are cleared (the placeholder goes), unless onTop is set.
onTop The structure goes on top of what is already there. The cells are not cleared.

A Fill anchor with neither object list nor loot table stays empty and logs a warning. A Fill anchor with a loot table but no object list drops the loot loose on the floor near the anchor.

Pieces can also have content zones (contentZones, see ContentZoneSpec). This is the older way to add loot. Zones use the same object lists and loot tables. A zone without its own lootTable takes the lootTable of the settlement's BudgetEntry for that piece or its category.

Object lists

Node: <CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef>. An object list names the structures an anchor may place.

<CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef MayRequire="DankPyon.Medieval.Overhaul">
  <defName>CBF_MO_Barrel</defName>
  <members>
    <li>DankPyon_HideoutRusticBarrelClosed</li>
    <li>DankPyon_HideoutCrate1x1c</li>
  </members>
</CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef>

A member can be a plain defName, as above, or a block with more nodes:

<CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef MayRequire="DankPyon.Medieval.Overhaul">
  <defName>CBF_MO_WeaponRack</defName>
  <members>
    <li><thing>DankPyon_HideoutRackSword</thing><foreign>true</foreign></li>
    <li><thing>DankPyon_HideoutRackSpear</thing><foreign>true</foreign></li>
    <li><thing>DankPyon_HideoutRackMixed</thing><foreign>true</foreign></li>
  </members>
</CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef>

Member nodes

Node Meaning
thing defName of the structure.
lootTable A loot table for this member only. It replaces the anchor's table. If the table does not exist, the anchor's table is used and a warning is logged.
empty What the structure becomes once emptied. On a shelf or other open structure, this building takes its place. On a shell (see below), only its picture is used. Must have the same footprint as thing, otherwise a warning is logged and the swap does not happen.
stays The structure itself is placed and stays as it is. Loot lies visibly on it or goes into it.
foreign CBF places the structure and does nothing else. Loot, occupants and the empty look come from the mod that owns it.

A shipped example of stays:

<CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef MayRequire="DankPyon.Medieval.Overhaul">
  <defName>CBF_MO_WritingSet</defName>
  <members>
    <li><thing>DankPyon_OnTop_Scribe_Book</thing><stays>true</stays></li>
    <li><thing>DankPyon_OnTop_Scribe_ScrollAndInkwell</thing><stays>true</stays></li>
    <li><thing>DankPyon_OnTop_Scribe_StackPaper</thing><stays>true</stays></li>
  </members>
</CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef>

What gets placed

What CBF places depends on the kind of building and the member flags.

  • CBF container: placed as is. It gets the loot table and rolls it when it spawns.
  • Other container (any Building_Casket, for example a Medieval Overhaul lootable chest) without stays or foreign: CBF places a shell of the same size instead. A shell is a CBF container that looks like the original, takes its label and description, and holds the loot inside until it is opened. If the member has empty, the shell shows that building's picture once opened.
  • Storage (a shelf, Building_Storage): placed as is. The loot lies on its cells, respecting the shelf's storage filter and items per cell.
  • Anything else, for example a table: placed as is. The loot lies on its cells.
  • With foreign, or on an anchor with lootForeign, none of the above applies. The structure is placed and left alone.

Loot on a structure's cells goes to the cell with the fewest items. A shelf allows its maxItemsInCell, anything else one stack per cell. When every cell is full, the rest of that item is not created. Items a shelf does not accept go onto free floor within 4 cells of the anchor. So does loot that does not fit into a container.

What it becomes when emptied

For structures that hold loot on their cells, CBF checks every 250 ticks whether the loot is gone.

  • With empty: the structure is replaced by the empty building, same position and rotation.
  • Without empty, on a shelf: the shelf stays.
  • Without empty, on anything else: the structure vanishes.
  • With stays or foreign: nothing is checked, the structure stays.
  • Structures owned by the player, or on a map owned by the player, are never swapped or removed.

CBF containers handle this themselves, see Containers.

Pawn inside

An object list can hide a pawn in its containers, for example an animal in a chest. This only works for buildings with an inside (caskets and CBF containers), not on shelves. This example is written for this page.

<CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef>
  <defName>MyMod_TrappedChest</defName>
  <members>
    <li>MyMod_Chest</li>
  </members>
  <pawnList>MyMod_ChestAnimals</pawnList>
  <pawnChance>0.15</pawnChance>
  <pawnCount>1</pawnCount>
  <pawnHostile>true</pawnHostile>
</CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef>
Node Meaning
pawnList defName of a PawnListDef, a weighted list of pawn kinds.
pawnChance Chance per container. Default 0, so nobody is inside unless set.
pawnCount How many, as a range. Default 1.
pawnHostile The pawn attacks once the container is opened. Animals go manhunter, people go berserk. A pawn of a faction hostile to the player fights for its faction instead. Handled by CBF containers and shells.
pawnFaction FactionDef of the occupant. Without it, a person that is not hostile joins the settlement's faction.

In a CBF container, a hostile occupant acts as a guard. Opening the container first releases the guard. The container cannot be searched while the guard is alive and outside.

Kind

kind takes Fill (default) or Empty. With Empty the structures are placed without loot and without occupants, and Medieval Overhaul lootables do not fill themselves either.

Object size and anchor area

The anchor area is the set of cells drawn with the anchor symbol. Only members that fit into the free cells of that area can be picked. A member may be placed turned by 90 degrees if its rotatable is true.

  • Among the members that fit, the pick is weighted by footprint. A 2x1 shelf is twice as likely as a 1x1 crate.
  • CBF places the structure as close to the middle of the area as it fits.
  • Rotation and material come from the placeholder building drawn under the anchor, if there is one and the material fits.
  • Water cells never count as free.
  • If no member fits: with a loot table, the loot is dropped loose near the anchor. Without one, the drawn placeholder stays and a warning is logged.

In dev mode, CBF warns when only one member of a list fits an area, because then the same structure always appears.

Other mods

Use MayRequire on the list or on a member so the XML loads without that mod.

<CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef>
  <defName>CBF_Vanilla_ShieldGenerator_High</defName>
  <members>
    <li MayRequire="Ludeon.RimWorld.Royalty"><thing>ShieldGeneratorMortar</thing><stays>true</stays></li>
  </members>
</CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef>

A member whose thing does not exist is skipped. If no member is left, the list is empty and every anchor that uses it is cleared and stays empty. If the list itself does not exist, the anchor stays empty and a warning is logged.

Loot tables

Node: <CustomBaseFramework.LootTableDef>. A loot table decides what comes out. Each entry is a single item, another table or a whole thing category.

A table of tables (shortened):

<CustomBaseFramework.LootTableDef>
  <defName>CBF_MO_Loot_Dwelling</defName>
  <label>dwelling loot</label>
  <rolls>2~4</rolls>
  <uniqueEntries>true</uniqueEntries>
  <emptyFallback>CBF_MO_Loot_Minimal</emptyFallback>
  <entries>
    <li><weight>20</weight><table>CBF_MO_Goods_Bread</table></li>
    <li><weight>16</weight><table>CBF_MO_Goods_Cheese</table></li>
    <li><weight>12</weight><table>CBF_MO_Goods_Textiles</table></li>
    <li><weight>8</weight><table>CBF_MO_Goods_Wine</table></li>
  </entries>
</CustomBaseFramework.LootTableDef>

This draws 2 to 4 different goods tables, bread most often. If nothing comes out, CBF_MO_Loot_Minimal is rolled instead.

A table of items (shortened):

<CustomBaseFramework.LootTableDef>
  <defName>CBF_Vanilla_Goods_Garments</defName>
  <label>garments goods</label>
  <rolls>1~2</rolls>
  <entries>
    <li><weight>20</weight><thing>Apparel_BasicShirt</thing><count>1~3</count><quality>Awful~Good</quality><hitPoints>0.4~0.9</hitPoints><onMissing>Skip</onMissing></li>
    <li><weight>12</weight><thing>Apparel_Jacket</thing><count>1~2</count><quality>Awful~Good</quality><hitPoints>0.4~0.9</hitPoints><onMissing>Skip</onMissing></li>
    <li MayRequire="Ludeon.RimWorld.Royalty"><weight>2</weight><thing>Apparel_Corset</thing><count>1</count><quality>Normal~Excellent</quality><onMissing>Skip</onMissing></li>
  </entries>
</CustomBaseFramework.LootTableDef>

Table nodes

Node Meaning
rolls How many entries are drawn from entries. Default 1~3.
rollChance Chance that the draw happens at all. One check for the whole draw, not per roll. If it fails, only always entries come. Default 1.
uniqueEntries Each entry can be drawn at most once per draw. Default false.
maxEntriesPerResolve Stops further weighted draws once the result reaches this many item-selection records. Default 16. This is not a hard cap: always entries and a nested table can exceed it. It does not count final stacks or individual items.
always Entries that are taken every time, each after its own chance check. They do not use a draw.
entries Entries drawn by weight.
emptyFallback Another table, rolled if this one produced nothing. It may not point to itself.

The initial table is evaluated at recursion depth 0. Nested tables and fallbacks share a limit of four additional levels; calls beyond depth 4 return no results. Avoid circular table references even though this limit prevents endless recursion.

Entry kinds

Each entry sets exactly one of thing, table or category. An entry in entries with none of them is a config error. If more than one is set, table wins over category, and category wins over thing.

Kind Node What happens
Item thing That ThingDef.
Table table The named table is rolled tableRolls times (default 1) and everything it gives is added.
Category category A ThingCategoryDef. One thing from the category and its subcategories is picked, weighted by the thing's generateCommonality.

A category entry, written for this page:

<li>
  <weight>4</weight>
  <category>Drugs</category>
  <maxTechLevel>Medieval</maxTechLevel>
  <value>5~60</value>
  <count>1~3</count>
</li>

For categories, only things the game allows in random item sets are used. maxTechLevel drops things above that tech level. value drops things whose base market value lies outside the range. The default 0~0 means no limit. If nothing is left, the entry gives nothing. Things made from stuff get a random fitting stuff.

Entry nodes

Node Meaning
weight Weight against the other entries in entries. Default 1. An entry with weight 0 is never drawn.
chance Only used in always: the chance that the entry is taken. Default 1.
count Quantity for a direct item or category result. After depth scaling, at least one item is produced. Placement splits quantities into stacks as needed. This field does not multiply nested-table results; use tableRolls for those.
quality Quality range, for example Poor~Good. Picked evenly within the range. Only for things that have quality. Default Awful~Legendary.
hitPoints Condition as a fraction of max hit points, for example 0.5~1.0. Default 1~1.
stuff Stuff for a thing made from stuff. If it is missing or not a stuff, the thing's default stuff is used.
maxTechLevel Category entries only, see above.
value Category entries only, see above.
tableRolls Table entries only, see above.
weightOverWealth A curve over the wealth of the player's home map. The weight is multiplied by it.
requiresAnyMod A list of packageIds. The entry is only used if at least one of them is active.
onMissing What happens if thing does not exist: Skip, Fallback or Silver. Default Skip.
fallbackThings Used with onMissing Fallback. The first one that exists replaces thing.

The depth fields are evaluated at 0 for settlements. In the weighted entries list, a depthRange excluding 0 prevents selection. In always, the current code only applies the depth/weight rejection when weightOverDepth is present. Use chance to control ordinary always entries. weightOverDepth replaces the base weight, whereas weightOverWealth multiplies it.

When a mod is missing

There are three ways to handle things from other mods.

  1. MayRequire on the li. The entry is not loaded at all. This is what the shipped tables use.
  2. requiresAnyMod. The entry loads but is ignored unless one of the mods is active.
  3. onMissing for a thing that does not exist:
    • Skip: the entry is dropped and a warning is logged once.
    • Fallback: the first existing defName from fallbackThings is used. If none exists, the entry is dropped.
    • Silver: silver is used instead, with the entry's count.
<li>
  <weight>6</weight>
  <thing>MyMod_Candle</thing>
  <count>2~5</count>
  <onMissing>Fallback</onMissing>
  <fallbackThings>
    <li>DankPyon_Candle</li>
    <li>Candle</li>
  </fallbackThings>
</li>

A table or category entry that points to something that does not exist is dropped with a warning. If no entry of a table is usable, the table stays empty and CBF logs it. An anchor that names an empty table places its structure without loot.

Containers

A CBF container is a building that holds loot inside, looks different once searched, and can turn into another building or vanish. Use it when a plain shelf is not enough.

CBF ships an abstract base, CBF_BeutebehaelterBase, in Defs/CustomBaseFramework/ThingDefs/CBF_Beutebehaelter.xml. It sets thingClass to CustomBaseFramework.Building_LootContainer, the contents tab and the usual building values. Inherit from it and add a size, a graphic and the extension. The containers for Medieval Overhaul are in Defs/CustomBaseFramework/ThingDefs/CBF_MO_Freigabe_Behaelter.xml. This one is shipped:

<ThingDef ParentName="CBF_BeutebehaelterBase" MayRequire="DankPyon.Medieval.Overhaul">
  <defName>CBF_MO_ArmorStand_White</defName>
  <label>armor stand</label>
  <description>A stand with a suit of armor and a helmet. Someone could make use of them.</description>
  <size>(1,1)</size>
  <defaultPlacingRot>South</defaultPlacingRot>
  <graphicData>
    <texPath>Things/Building/Art/ArmorStatue</texPath>
    <graphicClass>Graphic_Multi</graphicClass>
    <drawSize>(2,2)</drawSize>
  </graphicData>
  <costList>
    <DankPyon_IronIngot>40</DankPyon_IronIngot>
  </costList>
  <statBases>
    <MaxHitPoints>120</MaxHitPoints>
    <Flammability>0.2</Flammability>
  </statBases>
  <modExtensions>
    <li Class="CustomBaseFramework.LootContainerExtension">
      <emptyGraphic>
        <texPath>Things/Building/CBF/SockelWeiss</texPath>
        <graphicClass>Graphic_Multi</graphicClass>
        <drawSize>(2,2)</drawSize>
      </emptyGraphic>
    </li>
  </modExtensions>
</ThingDef>

Load your content mod after CBF so RimWorld can resolve this abstract parent. Keep the dependency in About.xml; copying the parent into your mod would also copy maintenance responsibility for its settings.

Extension nodes

CustomBaseFramework.LootContainerExtension:

Node Meaning
emptyGraphic A GraphicData shown once the container has been searched. It stays the same container.
emptyBuilding A ThingDef that replaces the container once searched. Needs the same footprint, otherwise the container stays and a warning is logged.
destroyWhenEmpty The container vanishes once searched, leaving nothing behind.
searchSound A SoundDef played when it is opened.
searchEffect A FleckDef shown when it is opened.
effectSize Size of that fleck. Default 1.5.

emptyBuilding and destroyWhenEmpty exclude each other. Setting both is a config error.

Empty picture only:

<modExtensions>
  <li Class="CustomBaseFramework.LootContainerExtension">
    <emptyGraphic>
      <texPath>Things/Building/Ruin/RuinedShelf</texPath>
      <graphicClass>Graphic_Multi</graphicClass>
      <drawSize>(2.75,2.75)</drawSize>
    </emptyGraphic>
  </li>
</modExtensions>

How it behaves

  • The picture changes when the container is opened, not when it runs out of items.
  • The loot table comes from the anchor or the member, never from the container def. A container placed by hand stays empty. In dev mode with god mode, a button on the container lets you fill it from any table for testing.
  • The contents are rolled once, when the container spawns.
  • A shell takes its empty picture from the member's empty building, or else from an empty graphic declared in the original building's own mod extension. A shell never turns into another building.
  • A container of your own takes its empty picture from emptyGraphic.

Shelves with visible loot

To show loot lying on a vanilla or modded shelf, do not use a CBF container. Put the shelf in an object list. CBF places the shelf itself and lays the items on its cells. When the items are gone, the shelf stays, or turns into the member's empty building.

<CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef>
  <defName>CBF_VFEM2_Shelf</defName>
  <members>
    <li>Shelf</li>
  </members>
</CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef>

A mod's own self-filling shelf is marked foreign, so CBF does not touch its contents:

<CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef>
  <defName>CBF_VFEM2_AutoShelf</defName>
  <members>
    <li><thing>VFEM2_AutofillShelf</thing><foreign>true</foreign></li>
  </members>
</CustomBaseFramework.LootBuildingCategoryDef>

Medieval Overhaul lootables

Medieval Overhaul lootables fill themselves when they spawn. CBF blocks that while it places one with a CBF loot table, and on lists of kind Empty. With foreign, or on an anchor with lootForeign, the lootable keeps its own loot.

Crop lists

Node: <CustomBaseFramework.CropListDef>. A crop list is a weighted list of plants for fields and pens. CBF ships none, so this example is written for this page.

<CustomBaseFramework.CropListDef>
  <defName>MyMod_Crops_Grain</defName>
  <label>grain</label>
  <plants>
    <li><plant>Plant_Rice</plant><weight>3</weight></li>
    <li><plant>Plant_Corn</plant><weight>2</weight></li>
    <li><plant>Plant_Potato</plant></li>
  </plants>
</CustomBaseFramework.CropListDef>
Node Meaning
plant defName of a plant. Entries that do not exist or are not plants are skipped with a warning.
weight Weight against the other plants. Default 1. A weight of 0 or less is a config error and the entry is skipped.

A list without plants is a config error.

A field or pen names the list in crops. Fields sit in the fields list of a district (or of a ward), pens in pens. See Fields and pens.

<fields>
  <li>
    <count>1~2</count>
    <crops>MyMod_Crops_Grain</crops>
    <plants>
      <li>Plant_Rice</li>
    </plants>
  </li>
</fields>

If crops names a list that exists and has at least one usable plant, the list is used and plants is ignored. If the list does not exist or has no usable plant, the field falls back to plants, and each of them is equally likely.

Tips

  • Build small tables of goods and combine them in larger tables with table entries. The shipped tables work this way.
  • Use emptyFallback to provide a second table when the first produces no results. Ensure the fallback has usable entries; an empty or unavailable fallback cannot guarantee loot.
  • Use MayRequire on every entry and member from another mod. A list whose members are all missing clears its anchors.
  • Make anchor areas the size of the structures you want. If only one member fits, the same structure always appears.
  • Draw a placeholder building under each anchor. It sets the rotation and material of what is placed.
  • Mark a mod's own lootables foreign if their author's loot should stay. Leave the flag off to use your own table.
  • Use Sample in the Loot tab to inspect the saved table and its nested tables, then place a test container to check the generated contents.
