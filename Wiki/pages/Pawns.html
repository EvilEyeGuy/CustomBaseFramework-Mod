Pawns

Pawn

Use the Pawn tab to design individual pawn kinds and combine them into reusable lists for settlement inhabitants, defenders and animals.

  • A pawn kind is a type of pawn with its gear, skills and traits, for example a crossbowman. RimWorld generates each pawn fresh from its kind.
  • A pawn list is a weighted set of pawn kinds with a role, for example the guards at a gate. Garrisons, Pawn anchors in pieces, animal pens and containers with someone inside draw their pawns from pawn lists.

The chips at the top switch between Mod (only what your mod contains) and All mods. Pawn kinds from other mods can be viewed but not changed in place. Copy it to edit at the bottom right copies one into your mod. Pawn kind from selection copies the kind of the pawn selected on the map into your mod.

Pawn kinds

  1. Portrait of an example pawn. Front, Side and Back turn it, Roll a new one generates another example. The portrait updates with every change.
  2. Key figures: race, combat power, tech level and apparel budget. The tech level comes from the most advanced gear, and the line names that piece of gear. It decides in which settlements the kind appears, see Pawn lists.
  3. Gear grid: apparel layers in rows, from outside in, and body parts in columns. Click a free cell to open a picker with only what fits there. Hatched cells are blocked by the gear of the same colour. hide empty hides rows and columns nobody uses.
  4. Gear list: each piece of gear with layer, material, quality and colour. Swap replaces a piece with another one that fits the same layers. Material, quality and colour stay where possible.
  5. Right side: Weapon (tags, budget, quality, material), Skills, Nature (forbidden and forced traits, with degree), Implants, and All fields for everything else.
  6. Place pawn puts one on the map. Add to pawn list adds the kind to a list.

The line at the bottom left runs the same check the game runs when it loads. As long as it finds errors, Save is blocked, and the line names the reason.

Pawn lists

  1. Members: the pawn kinds of the list as tiles, with portrait, tech level, weight (Wt.) and share. The higher the weight, the more often the kind is drawn. + Pawn kind opens a picker with portraits. You can pick several kinds at once and filter by People or Animals and by mod. Kinds already in the list are hidden. With Ideology active, the R button on a tile gives pawns of that kind a role of their faction's ideoligion: a role such as a moral guide, or Combat role by weapon for the shooting or melee specialist, depending on the weapon.
  2. Role: how the pawns behave.
    • Guard: joins the garrison of the settlement, does not flee alone and moves out against attackers.
    • Post: holds its spot and defends it. Does not move out and does not fall back. For towers and gatehouses.
    • Gunner: mans the nearest artillery piece and stays there.
    • Civilian: stays nearby and runs away when it gets dangerous. For innkeepers, market folk and farmers.
  3. Radius: how far the pawns spread around their spot.
  4. Used by: everything that names this list, such as settlement types, pieces and object lists. Click one to open it.

Tech level on the right decides which members a settlement uses. With from gear, each member counts at the tech level of its weapons and apparel. A settlement then uses the members with the highest tech level at or below its own. Members without a tech level always count. One list of guards can therefore serve every age: a medieval town gets the crossbowmen, an industrial town the riflemen.

A fixed tech level makes the settlement draw from the whole list without looking at the gear. Use it when the gear does not fit, for example tribal warriors in medieval leather who should appear in a tribal camp.
