Pawns

The Pawn tab edits pawn kinds and pawn lists. Pawn lists are what garrisons, Pawn anchors and pens use.

The chips at the top switch between Mod (only what your mod contains) and All mods. Kinds from other mods can be viewed and copied into your mod, not changed in place. Pawn kind from selection copies the kind of the pawn selected on the map.

Pawn kinds

  1. Portrait of an example pawn, turnable. It updates with every change.
  2. Key figures: combat power and the tech level, with the reason for it.
  3. Gear grid: layers from outside in, body parts across. A free cell opens a picker with only what fits there. Hatched cells are blocked by the piece of the same color. hide empty hides rows and columns nobody uses.
  4. Gear list: each piece with layer, stuff, quality and color. Swap replaces a piece with another that fits the same layers and keeps stuff, quality and color where possible.
  5. Right side: weapon tags and budget, skills, traits (forbidden and forced, with degree), implants, and All fields for everything else.
  6. Place pawn puts one on the map. Add to pawn list adds the kind to a list.

The check at the bottom is the same one the game runs when it loads. As long as it finds errors, saving is blocked and the reason is shown.

Pawn lists

  1. Members as tiles with portrait, weight and share. + Pawn kind opens a tile picker with portraits. Pick several at once, filter by People or Animals and by mod. Kinds already in the list are hidden.
  2. Role: Guard patrols, Post holds its position, Gunner mans artillery, Civilian flees.
  3. Radius: how far they spread around their anchor.
  4. Tech level: from gear works the level out from each member's weapons and apparel, see below. A fixed level makes the settlement draw from the whole list, for example tribal warriors in medieval leather for a tribal camp.
  5. Used by: the settlements and pieces that use this list.

A list uses the members with the highest tech level at or below the tech level of the settlement. Kinds without a tech level always count. One list of guards can therefore serve every age.
