Pieces

A piece is one building or one stretch of wall, drawn on the map and saved as a PieceDef. Houses, workshops, towers, gatehouses and wall segments are all pieces.

  1. List grouped by category. The chips filter by size (small up to 8 cells, medium 9 to 14, large 15 and more) or show templates only. All mods below them shows only the pieces of one mod; pieces from a subfolder such as Mods/MedievalOverhaul are listed separately. Right-click an entry to rename, duplicate, mark as template or delete it.
  2. Image drawn from the piece's data: floor, walls, furniture and the anchors on top. The dashed line is the outline of the piece. Small tiles elsewhere in the editor show anchors by color only, without names.
  3. Basics. Size, categories, rotation, clearing the footprint, and for buildings At the wall and Weight.
  4. Anchors of the piece and the details of the selected one.
  5. Check runs the same check the game runs on load. Click the result at the bottom left to see every finding. Save writes the piece back into its file.

Draw a new piece

A piece is drawn in the normal game, on the map of your colony, and then taken into the editor.

  1. Build the house first, with the normal architect tools, in god mode if you like. Build it exactly as it should appear: the walls, floors, doors, furniture and stuff you place are what the piece will contain. Pick concrete things here, such as a wooden wall or a specific bed, because the export takes them as they stand.
  2. Click + New in the Piece tab. The editor folds away and the toolbox Piece on the map opens on the right.
  3. Paint area marks the cells that belong to the piece. The area does not have to be a rectangle. Cut takes cells out again.
  4. Empty cell marks cells that belong to the piece but stay empty, for example a yard.
  5. Give the piece a Name and pick a Category in the picker, or create a new one there. Without a category it cannot be exported.
  6. Check, then Export. The piece is written into your mod and appears in the list.
  7. Back to the editor opens the window again with the new piece selected.

A gatehouse needs two anchors named Wall and one named Outside. You set them in the image after the export, until then the check reports them as notes.

Change an existing piece

  • Place to edit puts the piece on the map so you can change its structure, then export it again. Its anchors are kept.
  • Place puts a copy on the map the way the generator would build it, with variants and chances rolled. Good for a look, not for exporting.

Anchors

An anchor marks cells where something happens once the piece stands: a wall docks on, loot appears, people wait.

  1. + Anchor creates an anchor and lets you drag its area in the image right away. The size shows while you drag.
  2. Paint anchors lays a cell grid over the image. Drag rectangles to add cells to the selected anchor. Right-click or Escape ends it.
  3. Erase anchors removes anchor cells inside a rectangle.
  4. From another piece copies all anchors of another piece with their settings, without areas.
  5. Each row has Area (drag it again), Copy (same settings, no area) and ×.

Anchor kinds

  • Connect ties the piece to its surroundings. The name decides what for:
  • Wall: the town wall docks here. Gatehouses need at least two cells.
  • Outside: which side faces out. Gatehouses need it.
  • Anbau: annexes attach here, see Annexes.
  • Loot places objects from an object list and fills them from a loot table. Loot decoration only leaves them empty, unchanged leaves them to the mod that brings them.
  • Object places objects from an object list with a chance, without loot.
  • Pawn places pawns from a pawn list, or a single pawn kind, with a count and a chance.

The ... button in front of a list opens a preview of what is in it.

  • An anchor whose list does not exist, or whose list holds nothing that fits the anchor area, marks the piece red in the list.
  • If only some objects of the list are too large for the area, the anchor shows a yellow note. Only what fits is placed.

Things from other mods

  • A thing drawn directly into the piece is required. If its mod or DLC is missing, the generator does not use the piece at all, because a missing wall or door would leave a hole.
  • A thing placed through an object list is optional. Entries whose mod is missing drop out. If nothing is left, nothing appears at the anchor and the rest of the piece is built. The placeholder drawn under the anchor may come from that mod; it does not block the piece.
  • So: draw walls, doors and anything the building needs directly. Put devices, furniture and decoration that only some mods bring into an object list.
