Scenarios

Playing

CBF adds scenarios in which your colony starts in the ruins of an abandoned settlement. The walls are broken, roofs have fallen in and trees grow through the floors. The buildings belong to no one, so you can claim and repair them. Something may still be sleeping inside. To build your own ruin scenario, see Starting in a ruin.

The scenarios

Scenario Needs Start
Ruins of a Town nothing Four settlers of a modern colony, with rifle, revolver, knife, survival meals, medicine, components, steel and wood. Wrecked war machines and rusted cars lie in front of the settlement.
Ruins of a Village Medieval Overhaul Four settlers of a medieval kingdom, with bow, hatchet, knife, rations, herbal medicine, basic components, iron and wood. A ruined siege tower, battering ram, carts, banners and fallen footmen lie in front of the village.

Both start with 300 silver. The settlers can be chosen from eight candidates.

Starting a game

  1. Choose the scenario and set up the world as usual.
  2. After you choose the starting site, an extra page Settlement ruin appears. Pick the settlement type of the ruin or keep Random. Only settlement types that fit the tech level of your faction are listed: medieval types for the village scenario, modern types for the town.
  3. On the same page you can set the map size. Large settlement types need a larger map, the page shows the minimum. A smaller value is not accepted.
  4. The map is generated with the settlement on it. This takes longer than a normal start.

Your settlers start just outside the settlement.

What you find

  • Buildings without owner. Every wall, door, bed and workbench is free to use. Claim doors and buildings to make them yours. Many are damaged and need repair.
  • Damage. Breaches in the walls, collapsed roofs, burnt rooms, rubble, a fallen town wall in places. Lights and torches are out.
  • No loot. The settlement was abandoned long ago. Containers are empty. What you get are the buildings themselves.
  • Corpses. A few skeletons of former residents and attackers.
  • Overgrowth. Trees and bushes grow in the streets and open spaces. Fields are gone, animals have run off or turned wild.
  • Sleepers. A group of hostile raiders and predators sleeps under a roof somewhere in the settlement, usually away from the map centre. They wake when one of your colonists comes close and sees them, or when there is noise nearby. Explore carefully and with weapons ready.

Adding a ruin start to other scenarios

The ruin start is a scenario part. In the scenario editor, add Start in a settlement ruin to any scenario and choose:

  • the settlement type, or random
  • the kind of ruin
  • who sleeps inside: nobody, animals, raiders or both
  • how strong the sleepers are

A scenario can only contain one ruin start.

Things to know

  • The settlement is built in the style of a faction in your world that uses this settlement type. If no faction uses a matching type, the list is empty and the map has no settlement.
  • Mods that reshape the map can affect the ruin in the same way as other CBF settlements, see Compatibility.
  • If the starting map has no settlement, the log explains why. Look for lines starting with CBFStart or Startruine.
