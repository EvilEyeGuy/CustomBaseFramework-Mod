World Domination outposts

These settlement types load only with World Domination 2.0. World Domination gives each settlement a type such as farming or mining. The outposts are small camps for the types of tier 1 and 2, and war camps for vanguards at any tier. Their map is 175 cells.

Camps for quest sites

Quests in RimWorld send you to bandit camps, outposts and work sites. CBF builds these sites too, with the switch Build camps and work sites with CBF in the mod settings. The stockpile and workers of work sites stay as in the base game.

The map of a camp is 200 cells. A faction without a matching camp gets the site as RimWorld builds it.

What the quest already placed stays. If a quest puts a hideout or another building on the map, the camp is built around it. A camp on a quest site has no fixed garrison: its defenders come from the threat points of the site. A site that has no loot in the base game gets no loot from CBF either.

Defenders

  • Medieval Overhaul: bowmen, arbalesters, pikemen and knights.
  • VFE Medieval 2: archers, heavy archers, crossbowmen, halberdiers, men-at-arms and knights.
  • Modern: riflemen, snipers, assaulters, machine gunners, grenadiers, police and officers. Spacer and ultra factions field the spacer versions of the same roles, for example charge riflemen and marine assaulters.

The large town and the metropolis have sandbag positions with a mini turret or an autocannon along their sandbag and barricade line. In tests the metropolis had 42 to 59 of them. Neither has gatehouses: roads run through gaps in the line.

Searching a settlement

After a fight, opening every container by hand takes many clicks. Search site under Orders marks every closed container on the map for opening in one click, without dragging an area. Your colonists then open them with the normal open job.

  • The button only shows on CBF settlements. It is greyed out while hostile pawns are still standing; downed ones do not count.
  • Containers in unexplored rooms are skipped. The message says how many, so press again once they are explored.

Performance

Measured on 6 identical world tiles per type, compared with a vanilla faction base. The current version plans faster than the build these numbers come from.

Type Map generation Memory Save file Pawns on the map
Vanilla faction base 5.6 s +136 MB +8.8 MB 57
Village (MO) 21 s +135 MB +10 MB 103
Market town (MO) 17 s +148 MB +13 MB 150
Large market town (MO) 25 s +159 MB +16 MB 187
Fortress city (MO) 30 s +158 MB +16 MB 226
Modern town 10 s +147 MB +15 MB 110
Large town 16 s +168 MB +20 MB 157

Values are medians. Map generation happens once, when you first arrive at the settlement. It runs in the background for caravans, transport pods, shuttles and gravships, so the game does not freeze on arrival.

While playing:

  • Colony without a CBF settlement: no measurable difference.
  • On a fortress city with 121 defenders: CBF's own code takes 0.05 to 0.09 % of the tick time.
  • World map: +0.1 ms per frame.
  • Game start: about +20 MB memory.
