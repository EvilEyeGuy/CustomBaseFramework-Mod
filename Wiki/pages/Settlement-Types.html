World Domination outposts

These settlement types load only with World Domination 2.0. They are small camps for the World Domination types of tier 1 and 2, and war camps for vanguards at any tier. The map is 175 cells.

Tier 3 and 4 use the large market town, fortress city, large town and metropolis. A World Domination settlement without a matching outpost gets the settlement type of its tier, see Compatibility.

Defenders

  • Medieval Overhaul: archers, crossbowmen, pikemen and knights.
  • VFE Medieval 2: the fighters of VFE Medieval 2.
  • Modern: riflemen, snipers, assaulters, machine gunners, grenadiers, police and officers. Spacer and ultra factions field the spacer versions of the same roles, for example charge riflemen and marine assaulters.

The metropolis adds 42 to 59 sandbag positions with mini turrets or autocannons. It has no gatehouses: roads run through gaps in the sandbag and barricade line.

Searching a settlement

Once no hostile pawn is left standing on a CBF settlement, Search site under Orders marks every closed container on the map for opening. It takes one click, no area. Your colonists then open them with the normal open job. Containers in unexplored rooms are skipped; the message says how many, so press again once they are explored. The button only shows on CBF settlements and is greyed out while hostiles are still standing.

Performance

Measured on 6 identical world tiles per type, compared with a vanilla faction base. The current version plans faster than the build these numbers come from.

Type Map generation Memory Save file Pawns on the map
Vanilla faction base 5.6 s +136 MB +8.8 MB 57
Village (MO) 21 s +135 MB +10 MB 103
Market town (MO) 17 s +148 MB +13 MB 150
Large market town (MO) 25 s +159 MB +16 MB 187
Fortress city (MO) 30 s +158 MB +16 MB 226
Modern town 10 s +147 MB +15 MB 110
Large town 16 s +168 MB +20 MB 157

Values are medians. Map generation happens once, when you first arrive at the settlement. It runs in the background for caravans, transport pods, shuttles and gravships, so the game does not freeze on arrival.

While playing:

  • Colony without a CBF settlement: no measurable difference.
  • On a fortress city with 121 defenders: CBF's own code takes 0.05 to 0.09 % of the tick time.
  • World map: +0.1 ms per frame.
  • Game start: about +20 MB memory.
